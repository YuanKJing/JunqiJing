abstract: Q-PhysJEPA introduces task queries before low-dimensional history compression in a
  joint-embedding predictive architecture. Visual, proprioceptive, and action histories are
  aggregated into evidence slots, reweighted through query-conditioned gating, and compressed
  into task-dependent representations. Candidate-conditioned latent future prediction and utility
  estimation connect these representations to decision-making. The study examines query-relative
  physical content through property readouts, predicted futures, and utility estimates, distinguishing
  accessible physical information from its usefulness for candidate selection.
summary: Q-PhysJEPA conditions history compression on the task query, learning compact representations
  that retain physical evidence relevant to the current objective.
