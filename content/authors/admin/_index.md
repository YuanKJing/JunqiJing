## About Me

I’m **Junqi Jing (荆浚淇)**, a B.E. candidate at **Harbin Institute of Technology** (Class of 2027) and an incoming Ph.D. student in SEEM at **The Chinese University of Hong Kong** (Fall 2027), advised by [**Prof. Liming Wang**](https://www.se.cuhk.edu.hk/people/academic-staff/prof-wang-liming/). I currently lead the Robot Interaction project as an AI Algorithm Intern at [**CoCoMatrix**](https://www.ccmtrx.com/), working with [Weikang Wan](https://wkwan7.github.io/). Previously, I interned at [**KNOWIN AI**](https://knowinai.com/) and conducted research at [**Tsinghua’s LEAP Lab**](https://www.leaplab.ai/) with [**Prof. Gao Huang**](https://www.au.tsinghua.edu.cn/info/1075/3183.htm) and [**POSTECH’s MLV Lab**](https://sites.google.com/view/mlvlab/home?authuser=0) with [**Prof. Kwang In Kim**](https://sites.google.com/view/kimki?pli=1&authuser=0). I have also been fortunate to receive guidance from [**Prof. Zhaobo Qi**](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/qizhaobo) during my undergraduate studies at HIT.

My research focuses on **world models, embodied agents, and self-improvement through interaction**. I’m interested in how robots learn from experience and human guidance to act more effectively in the physical world.

Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:xingkong8527@gmail.com) about research internships or collaborations!
