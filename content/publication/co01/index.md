abstract: >-
  CO01 is a robot model that adapts to new tasks from experience placed in its
  context, without task-specific retraining. It uses demonstrations, its own
  earlier attempts, and human guidance. An orchestrator maintains plans and
  longer-term memory, while a video-action policy interprets experience prompts
  and produces robot actions. Section 2.3 explores corrections during execution
  and the retention of human preferences.

Published in **October 2026** as COCO Matrix’s first technical blog.

**My contribution:** I was responsible for the work on **human guidance and preferences in Section 2.3, “From guidance.”** CO01 accepts corrections through speech, gestures, and scene changes while it acts. Interaction history preserves guidance within an episode, while the orchestrator stores preferences across sessions.
