Our first technical blog, [**Experience is the Prompt: Introducing CO01**](https://www.ccmtrx.com/research/co01/), is out! CO01 learns new tasks from demonstrations, its own attempts, and human guidance placed in context, without task-specific retraining.

I was responsible for the work on **human guidance and preferences in Section 2.3, “From guidance.”** This part explores how robots respond to spoken instructions, gestures, and changes to the scene while a task is underway, and retain the preferences those interactions convey. Examples include learning cup–coaster pairings from pointing and redirecting a cup when a person places a coaster mid-task.

I’m honored to have contributed to this work with the CO01 team. Read the [English technical blog](https://www.ccmtrx.com/research/co01/) or the [Chinese announcement on WeChat](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/7sa98ZEFRklpNy6oIkU1aw), and follow COCO Matrix for more updates.
